Privacy

Vlement J Rodrigues respects your privacy.

Information you provide

If you contact me by email about my artwork, exhibitions or other enquiries, I may receive your name, email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

How I use your information

Your information is used only to respond to your enquiry and to communicate with you about the subject of your enquiry or any subsequent enquiry you make.

Sharing your information

I do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

How long I keep your information

I keep correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, unless I need to retain it for legal or accounting reasons.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights concerning the personal information I hold about you, including the right to request access to your information and, where appropriate, ask for it to be corrected or deleted.

If you have a question about how your personal information is used, please contact me using the email address provided on this website.

Questions or concerns

If you have concerns about the way your personal information is handled, you can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
