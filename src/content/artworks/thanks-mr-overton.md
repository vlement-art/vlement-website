 Based on the "Overton window," It outlines the spectrum of ideas ranging from unthinkable and radical to acceptable, sensible, popular, and finally policy

THANKS!! Mr Overton explores the subtle connection between people, memory, and how perception can change. Through expressive brushwork and a restrained palette of blues, greys, and warm earth tones, the painting creates a contemplative atmosphere in which the boundary between observer and observed becomes a space for reflection
